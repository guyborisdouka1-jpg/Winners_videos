30 JOURS D'ÉCOUTE ANGLOPHONE – ACTE 3
NIVEAU BEGINNER – TEXTES 1 À 30

Mode d'emploi : pour chaque audio, copiez uniquement le texte en anglais qui se trouve sous la ligne « Texte X », sans le titre. Enregistrez le fichier sous le nom « Audio X ». Vitesse lente.
20 MONOLOGUES (une personne parle seule) et 10 DIALOGUES (une réplique par ligne, deux personnes qui se répondent). Pour les dialogues, utilisez si possible deux voix : une pour les lignes 1, 3, 5…, une autre pour les lignes 2, 4, 6…

----------------------------------------
Texte 1 – Hello, I'm Emma (MONOLOGUE)
----------------------------------------
Hello! My name is Emma. I am twenty-five years old. I am from London, in England. I live in a small flat with my cat. My cat's name is Toby. I am a nurse. I work in a big hospital. I like my job. I like music and books. My favourite colour is blue. Nice to meet you!

----------------------------------------
Texte 2 – My family (MONOLOGUE)
----------------------------------------
This is my family. My father's name is David. He is a teacher. My mother's name is Anne. She is a doctor. I have one brother and one sister. My brother is ten years old. My sister is fifteen. We live in a house in Manchester. We have a big garden and a dog. I love my family very much.

----------------------------------------
Texte 3 – My house (MONOLOGUE)
----------------------------------------
I live in a house in Dublin. My house has two floors. Downstairs, there is a kitchen and a living room. Upstairs, there are three bedrooms and a bathroom. My bedroom is small, but it is nice. There is a bed, a desk and a big window. From my window, I can see the park. I like my house.

----------------------------------------
Texte 4 – My morning (MONOLOGUE)
----------------------------------------
Every morning, I wake up at six o'clock. I take a shower and I brush my teeth. Then I get dressed. I have breakfast in the kitchen. I eat bread and eggs, and I drink a cup of tea. At seven thirty, I leave the house. I take the bus to work. I am never late!

----------------------------------------
Texte 5 – Asking for a phone number (DIALOGUE)
----------------------------------------
Hi! I'm Jack. What's your name?
I'm Sarah. Nice to meet you, Jack.
Nice to meet you too. How old are you, Sarah?
I'm twenty-four. And you?
I'm thirty. What's your phone number?
It's zero, seven, nine, four, two, five, one.
Sorry, can you repeat, please?
Of course. Zero, seven, nine, four, two, five, one.
Thank you! I'll call you tomorrow.
OK, bye!

----------------------------------------
Texte 6 – Days of the week (MONOLOGUE)
----------------------------------------
There are seven days in a week. From Monday to Friday, I go to work. On Monday, I have a meeting. On Wednesday, I go to the gym after work. On Friday evening, I watch a film with my friends. On Saturday, I go shopping. On Sunday, I stay at home and I rest. Sunday is my favourite day.

----------------------------------------
Texte 7 – Food I like (MONOLOGUE)
----------------------------------------
I love food! For breakfast, I like cereal with milk. For lunch, I usually eat a sandwich and an apple. For dinner, I like chicken with rice and vegetables. I don't like fish. I drink a lot of water every day. My favourite fruit is the banana. On Sundays, my mother makes a big cake. It is delicious!

----------------------------------------

Texte 9 – The weather (MONOLOGUE)
----------------------------------------
Today, it is cold in Edinburgh. The sky is grey and it is raining. I wear my coat and I take my umbrella. In Scotland, it rains a lot. In summer, it is warm and sunny. I love summer! In winter, it is very cold and sometimes it snows. What is the weather like in your city today?

----------------------------------------
Texte 10 – My clothes (MONOLOGUE)
----------------------------------------
Today, I am wearing a white shirt and black trousers. I am wearing black shoes too. At work, I wear a jacket. At the weekend, I wear jeans and a T-shirt. I like comfortable clothes. My favourite clothes are my blue jeans and my red jumper. In winter, I wear a hat and a scarf.

----------------------------------------
Texte 11 – In a new town (DIALOGUE)
----------------------------------------
Excuse me, are you new in town?
Yes, I am. I arrived last week.
Welcome! Do you need anything?
Yes. Is there a supermarket near here?
Yes, there is one on Main Street, next to the bank.
Great. And is there a library?
Yes, there is a big library near the park.
Thank you very much. You're very kind.
You're welcome. Enjoy our town!

----------------------------------------
Texte 12 – Going to work (MONOLOGUE)
----------------------------------------
I work in the city centre of Sydney. Every day, I go to work by train. The train station is near my house. I walk for five minutes. The journey takes twenty minutes. On the train, I read a book or I listen to music. Sometimes, when the weather is nice, I go to work by bike.

----------------------------------------
Texte 13 – My job (MONOLOGUE)
----------------------------------------
Hello, I'm Oliver. I am a chef. I work in a restaurant in London. I start work at ten in the morning and I finish at ten at night. It is a long day! I cook meat, fish and vegetables. I like my job because I love cooking. My colleagues are friendly. On Mondays, the restaurant is closed.

----------------------------------------

----------------------------------------

It's in aisle three, next to the cheese.

The bread is at the back of the shop.

Yes, of course.

Texte 15 – What time is it? (DIALOGUE)
----------------------------------------

It's half past eight.
Oh no! My class starts at nine.
Where is your class?
It's in the city centre.
Take the bus. It leaves at quarter to nine.
How long is the journey?
About ten minutes.
Thank you! I'm going now.

Texte 16 – My best friend (MONOLOGUE)
----------------------------------------
My best friend is Lucy. She is twenty-six years old. She is tall and she has long brown hair. She has green eyes. Lucy is very kind and funny. She works in a bank. We meet every Friday evening. We go to the cinema or we eat pizza together. Lucy is a very good friend.

----------------------------------------
Texte 17 – Free time (MONOLOGUE)
----------------------------------------
In my free time, I like doing many things. I like playing football with my friends. I like listening to music, and I love dancing. I don't like watching television. In the evening, I read books. At the weekend, I go swimming. Swimming is good for my health. What do you like doing in your free time?

----------------------------------------
Texte 18 – At the doctor's (DIALOGUE)
----------------------------------------
Good morning. How can I help you?
Good morning, doctor. I don't feel well.
What's the problem?
I have a headache and a sore throat.
Do you have a temperature?
Yes, a little.
I see. Open your mouth, please. Hmm, it's a cold.
What can I do?
Drink a lot of water, take this medicine and rest for two days.
Thank you, doctor.

Texte 19 – Asking for directions (DIALOGUE)
----------------------------------------
Excuse me, where is the train station, please?
Go straight on, then turn left at the bank.
Turn left at the bank. OK.
Then walk for two minutes. The station is on your right.
Is it far?
No, it's not far. About five minutes on foot.
Thank you very much!
You're welcome. Have a good day!

Texte 20 – My English class (MONOLOGUE)
----------------------------------------
I study English at a language school. My class is on Tuesday and Thursday evenings. There are ten students in my class. My teacher is from Canada. She is very patient. In class, we listen, we speak and we write. We learn new words every day. English is not easy, but I like it very much.

----------------------------------------
Texte 21 – My phone (MONOLOGUE)
----------------------------------------
I use my phone every day. In the morning, I check the weather and my messages. I call my mother every evening. I take a lot of photos with my phone. I also listen to music and podcasts. But at night, I don't use my phone. I put it on the table and I read a book before I sleep.

Texte 23 – My bedroom (MONOLOGUE)
----------------------------------------
This is my bedroom. It is not very big, but it is comfortable. My bed is next to the window. On the wall, there is a picture of New York. There is a desk with a lamp and a computer. My clothes are in the wardrobe. My books are on the shelf. I like my bedroom because it is quiet.

Texte 25 – Last weekend (MONOLOGUE)
----------------------------------------
Last weekend was great. On Saturday, I visited my grandparents. We had lunch together and we talked a lot. In the afternoon, I walked in the park with my dog. On Sunday, I watched a football match on television. My team won! In the evening, I cooked pasta for my family. It was a very nice weekend.

----------------------------------------
Texte 26 – Yesterday (MONOLOGUE)
----------------------------------------
Yesterday, I woke up late. I missed the bus, so I walked to work. I arrived at nine thirty. My boss was not happy! At lunch, I ate a sandwich with my colleagues. After work, I went to the gym. Then I went home, I had dinner and I watched a film. I was very tired.

----------------------------------------
Texte 27 – My holiday (MONOLOGUE)
----------------------------------------
Last summer, I went on holiday to Canada. I travelled by plane with my family. We stayed in a hotel in Vancouver for one week. The city was beautiful. We visited a big park and we saw the mountains. We ate in nice restaurants. The people were very friendly. It was my best holiday!

Texte 29 – My dream (MONOLOGUE)
----------------------------------------
I have a dream. I want to speak English very well. I want to travel to London, New York and Sydney. I want to work for an international company. That is why I study English every day. I listen to audios, I read and I practise with my friends. Little by little, I am making progress.

----------------------------------------
Texte 30 – Thirty days later (MONOLOGUE)
----------------------------------------
Thirty days ago, English was very difficult for me. I did not understand people when they spoke fast. Now, I listen every day. I understand more words and more sentences. I am more confident. I can introduce myself, talk about my day and order food in English. This is only the beginning. Well done, and keep going!
